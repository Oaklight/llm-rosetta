`../rosetta-stone-path.txt` is the silhouette traced from that file and reduced
to one closed outline; the marks in `../out/` are parametric simplifications of
it.

CC0 requires no attribution. Recording the chain is still worth doing, for two
reasons: it answers "where did this shape come from" without re-deriving it, and
it makes the licence claim checkable.

Caveat: a CC0 dedication covers what the uploader had the right to dedicate.
The Commons page says the file came from open clip art, whose library is CC0,
and records no photographer. If the shape ultimately descends from a specific
photograph, that photographer's rights are not addressed by this dedication.
No such claim is known; this is the reason the licence, not the age of the
artifact, is what the mark relies on.

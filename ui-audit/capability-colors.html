Tools colour options

Text, vision and reasoning stay as they are in Deep; only the tools dot changes. Each panel shows the 6 px dot, the dot with its name, the badge, and the picker trigger with all four lit. Blue went because it reads as the house accent and sits between the purple and the teal — the new colour has to be seen next to those two.

Recommendation: fuchsia. It is the only hue left that stands apart from purple and teal at 6 px, and it stays bright on black. Rose is a close second but borders the failure red; cyan collapses into the reasoning teal.
